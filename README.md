### I built AI good enough to pass medical certification. Now I build it for companies.

I'm a physicist. From 2017 to 2026 I led the AI of [Legit.Health](https://legit.health/): co-founder and CTO, then Chief AI Officer, and Technical Director & PRRC of its CE-marked medical device, certified class IIb under the EU MDR and used by 2,500+ clinicians. Before that I was a computer-vision researcher at TECNALIA (plant disease, histology, industry) and a BI analyst at BBVA.

Now I'm independent. I build models, data pipelines and evaluation for a few companies at a time, and I do the work myself.
